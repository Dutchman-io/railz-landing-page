Work on ZemFi

ZemFi helps people hold stablecoins, fund and withdraw in their own currency, and send money across borders from their phone. If you want to build that with us, this is where we list the roles we are hiring for.

Open roles

  • Community Manager

    Build and look after the ZemFi community: answer questions where our users gather, run the conversations and events that bring new people in, and carry what users tell you back to the team.

    Apply

  • Sales

    Bring ZemFi to the people and businesses who move money across borders: find them, show them how ZemFi works for them, and see them through to their first transactions.

    Apply

  • Content Creator

    Make the videos, posts and guides that explain stablecoins, payments and ZemFi simply, and grow our audience on the platforms our users spend their time on.

    Apply

How to apply

Email careers@zemfi.ng with the role in the subject line. Include:

  • your CV, or a link to your work (GitHub, portfolio, writing);
  • a few lines on what you would want to work on at ZemFi, and why.

We read every application and reply to each one, including when it is a no.

Don’t see your role?

Write anyway. If you think you could make ZemFi better, tell us how at careers@zemfi.ng.
