How a payment moves

Your currency in. Stablecoins across. Their bank out.

  1. 01

    You pay in your currency

    Fund the payment from your local or stablecoin balance. The rate and the fee are shown before you confirm.

  2. 02

    It crosses as a stablecoin

    The money travels as USDT or USDC, pegged to the US dollar, so its value holds while it’s on the way.

  3. 03

    It lands in their bank

    Your recipient gets a normal bank payment. They never need a wallet or to know about crypto.

Questions

Good to know before you start.

What does “stablecoin-backed” mean?

When you pay someone abroad or get paid from abroad, the money moves as USDT or USDC, digital dollars that hold a 1:1 value with the US dollar. You and your recipient still see bank payments at each end.

Does the person I pay need crypto?

No. They receive a regular payment into their bank account. The stablecoin part happens in between.

What do I need to get started?

The ZemFi app on your phone and a few details to verify who you are. Verification happens once, before your first transfer.

Will I know the fee before I send?

Yes. The exchange rate and the fee are shown before you confirm, so you know exactly what arrives.

Which countries can I send to?

The United Kingdom, the Eurozone, Brazil, Mexico, the United States, Canada and China, with more on the way.

Which stablecoins does ZemFi support?

USDT and USDC, on Tron, Ethereum, Polygon, Base, Arbitrum and BNB Smart Chain.

Can I buy stablecoins with local currency?

Yes. Buy USDT or USDC from your local balance, with the rate and the fee shown before you confirm.

Where do my withdrawals go?

To a bank account in your own name. Save it once and it’s ready next time.

What if my connection drops mid-transfer?

Just try again. ZemFi recognises the retry, so your money is never sent twice.

Is ZemFi CBN compliant?

Identity checks follow the Central Bank of Nigeria’s tiered KYC framework: your BVN and details are verified against NIBSS records, and higher tiers add your NIN, an ID document and proof of address. Payments, balances and custody are provided by a licensed, regulated payments partner.

How is my account protected?

Transfers need a code from your authenticator app, and the app opens with Face ID or your fingerprint.
